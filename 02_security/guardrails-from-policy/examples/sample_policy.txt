ACME CORPORATION — DATA CLASSIFICATION AND ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
Version 3.1 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Owner: Chief Information Security Officer

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This policy defines how ACME Corporation classifies, handles, and protects
information assets. It applies to all employees, contractors, vendors, and AI
systems acting on behalf of ACME Corporation.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. DATA CLASSIFICATION TIERS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

2.1 PUBLIC
Description: Information approved for unrestricted public disclosure.
Examples: Marketing materials, press releases, published product documentation.
Handling: No special controls required.

2.2 INTERNAL
Description: General business information not intended for external audiences.
Examples: Internal memos, org charts, meeting notes, project status updates.
Handling: Must not be shared outside ACME without authorization. Employee
  credentials required to access internal systems.

2.3 CONFIDENTIAL
Description: Sensitive business information where unauthorized disclosure could
  harm ACME or its customers.
Examples: Financial forecasts, customer contracts, HR records, product roadmaps,
  pricing strategies, M&A discussions.
Handling: Shared on a need-to-know basis. Encryption required in transit and at
  rest. Access logged and auditable.
PII present: Customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, billing addresses,
  credit card numbers.

2.4 RESTRICTED
Description: Highest sensitivity. Unauthorized disclosure causes severe harm.
Examples: AWS account credentials, API keys, encryption keys, social security
  numbers, health records (PHI/HIPAA), source code for core products.
Handling: Access requires explicit written approval from the CISO. Encryption
  with customer-managed KMS keys mandatory. No AI system may output Restricted
  data verbatim under any circumstances.
PII present: Social Security Numbers, passwords, AWS secret keys, bank account
  numbers, health information.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. PROHIBITED TOPICS AND ACTIVITIES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

AI systems deployed by ACME must refuse requests on the following topics:

3.1 Competitor Intelligence Gathering
  AI systems must not assist employees in harvesting non-public information about
  competitors, including pricing, internal org structures, or roadmaps obtained
  through improper means.

3.2 Legal Advice
  AI systems must not provide legal opinions, advice on pending litigation, or
  interpretation of contracts. Employees must consult ACME Legal.

3.3 Financial Investment Recommendations
  AI systems must not provide personal investment advice, stock recommendations,
  or guidance on trading ACME securities. This could constitute securities fraud.

3.4 Harassment and Discriminatory Content
  AI systems must not generate content that demeans individuals based on race,
  gender, religion, sexual orientation, national origin, disability, or age.

3.5 Violence and Threatening Content
  AI systems must never generate threats, incitement to violence, or content
  that could be used to harm individuals.

3.6 Credentials and Secrets Disclosure
  AI systems must never output AWS credentials, API keys, passwords, or any
  authentication secret, even if such data appears in context.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. PROHIBITED PHRASES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The following codewords relate to unannounced products and must not appear in
AI-generated output or user inputs processed by AI systems:

  - Project Nighthawk
  - Operation Stardust
  - Codename Polaris

Profanity and hate speech are strictly prohibited in all AI interactions.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. ADVERSARIAL INPUT PROTECTION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ACME operates a zero-tolerance policy for prompt injection attacks. AI systems
must be configured to detect and block attempts to override instructions,
exfiltrate data, or bypass safety controls via adversarial inputs. This includes
direct injection, indirect injection via documents, role-play jailbreaks, and
encoding-based obfuscation attacks.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. FACTUAL ACCURACY REQUIREMENTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

AI systems used in customer-facing contexts must only produce responses grounded
in the provided knowledge base. Responses that are not grounded in retrieved
source material must be blocked. Required grounding threshold: 0.75.
Required relevance threshold: 0.70.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. INCIDENT REPORTING
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Any suspected data breach, policy violation, or AI safety incident must be
reported to security@acme.example within 24 hours.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. COMPLIANCE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

This policy aligns with: SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA (for health
data processors), and NIST AI RMF.

END OF POLICY
