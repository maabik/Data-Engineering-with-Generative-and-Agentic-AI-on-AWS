# Press Release — Acme Corp to Acquire FinFlow Inc

AUSTIN, TX — Acme Corp (NASDAQ: ACME) today announced a definitive
agreement to acquire FinFlow Inc for $185M in a mixed cash-and-stock
transaction. The acquisition is expected to close in the fourth fiscal
quarter of 2025, subject to customary regulatory approvals.

"FinFlow's payment-rails technology and engineering team led by Daniel
Park accelerate our financial-services product roadmap by at least 18
months," said Acme CEO Robert Lee.

FinFlow CEO Maria Chen will join Acme as SVP, Financial Services after
close. Daniel Park will lead the new Acme Payments division. Aisha
Patel will return to advising mode after the integration period.

The acquisition was advised by Goldman Sachs (Acme) and Qatalyst
Partners (FinFlow).
