# FinFlow Inc — Executive Bios

## Maria Chen, CEO and Co-founder

Maria Chen co-founded FinFlow in 2018. Before founding FinFlow she spent
seven years at Sequoia Capital as a principal focused on fintech
investments. Earlier in her career she was a product lead at Stripe and
a software engineer at Square. She holds a BS in Computer Science from
MIT.

## Daniel Park, CTO and Co-founder

Daniel Park leads engineering at FinFlow. Before FinFlow he was the
founding engineer at Plaid and an early engineer at Robinhood. He has
deep expertise in payment rails and ACH infrastructure. He earned an MS
in Computer Science from Stanford.

## Aisha Patel, CFO

Aisha Patel joined FinFlow in 2022 as Chief Financial Officer. She
previously held senior finance roles at Adyen and PayPal. She is a CPA
and an MBA graduate of Wharton.
