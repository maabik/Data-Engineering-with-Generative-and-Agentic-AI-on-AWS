# Acme Corp — 10-K Excerpt (FY2025)

Acme Corp is a publicly traded enterprise SaaS company headquartered in
Austin, Texas. The company reported total revenue of $412M for FY2025,
up 24% year over year. ARR ended the year at $480M.

The company's principal product line is the Acme Cloud Platform, a
multi-tenant data integration suite serving mid-market and enterprise
customers across financial services, healthcare, and logistics.

In Q3 FY2025, Acme Corp announced the acquisition of FinFlow Inc, a
fintech-focused payment-rails startup based in San Francisco, for $185M
in cash and stock. Acme expects FinFlow's payment infrastructure to
expand its financial-services product line.

Risks disclosed include: customer concentration (the top 10 customers
represent 31% of ARR), reliance on AWS as a sole cloud provider, and
ongoing litigation with a former engineering executive.
