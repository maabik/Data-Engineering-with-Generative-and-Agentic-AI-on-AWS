# FinFlow Inc — Sample Customer Contract Summary

## MegaBank N.A. — Master Services Agreement (2023)

FinFlow Inc and MegaBank N.A. entered into a three-year Master Services
Agreement on November 1, 2023, covering payment-rails integration and
ACH processing.

Key commercial terms:
- Total contract value: $42M over three years
- Annual minimum commit: $12M
- Termination for convenience: 90 days notice with 50% remaining-term penalty
- Change-of-control clause: MegaBank may terminate without penalty if
  FinFlow undergoes a change of control to a competitor of MegaBank's
  treasury services group.

The change-of-control clause is material because Acme Corp's existing
banking partnership with FirstUnion Bank may bring this clause into play
post-acquisition.
