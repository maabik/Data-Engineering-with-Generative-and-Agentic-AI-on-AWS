# Industry News — Acme Corp (2026 Roundup)

**February 2026** — Acme Corp announced general availability of Acme
Payments (formerly FinFlow Pay), launched 14 months after the FinFlow
acquisition closed.

**March 2026** — Linda Okafor, Acme's Chief Revenue Officer, departed to
become CEO of competitor DataPress Co. DataPress is a Sequoia-backed
data observability company.

**April 2026** — Acme reported Q1 FY2026 results: revenue of $128M
(+31% YoY); ACME stock down 11% post-earnings on softer guidance.

**May 2026** — A second former engineering executive, Karen Diaz, joined
the Webb litigation as a co-plaintiff. Diaz left Acme in early 2024 and
had been at the company since 2018.
