# Acme Corp — Pending Litigation Summary

## Webb v. Acme Corp (Travis County District Court, 2024)

A former Acme engineering vice president, James Webb, filed a wrongful
termination suit in 2024 alleging that his October 2023 separation
violated whistleblower protections. Webb claims he raised concerns
about the security posture of the data integration platform. Acme has
denied the allegations. Discovery is ongoing as of FY2025 close.

## In re: Acme Customer Data Incident (FTC Inquiry, 2024)

The FTC opened an inquiry in March 2024 regarding a data exposure
incident affecting approximately 12,000 Acme Cloud Platform customers.
Acme has cooperated fully. No formal action has been taken to date.

## Risk Implication

Both matters bear on the integration risk for the FinFlow acquisition,
particularly because FinFlow's payment-rails infrastructure will share
engineering resources with the existing platform once integrated.
