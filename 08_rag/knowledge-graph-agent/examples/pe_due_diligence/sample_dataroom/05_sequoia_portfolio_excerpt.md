# Sequoia Capital — Active Portfolio Excerpt (Public)

Selected Sequoia Capital portfolio companies, current and historical:

- **Stripe** — payments infrastructure. Sequoia first invested in 2010.
- **Plaid** — fintech APIs. Sequoia led Series B in 2017.
- **Robinhood** — retail brokerage. Sequoia led Series C in 2017.
- **FinFlow Inc** — payment-rails fintech. Sequoia led Series A in 2019.
- **Acme Corp** — enterprise SaaS. Sequoia exited in 2020 IPO.
- **DataPress Co** — data observability. Sequoia led Series B in 2022.

Maria Chen of FinFlow was a Sequoia principal from 2011 to 2018 before
leaving to co-found FinFlow.
