# Acme Corp — Executive Org Chart (Pre-Acquisition)

- **Robert Lee** — CEO. Joined Acme as CEO in 2019. Formerly COO at
  ServiceNow and a partner at Sequoia Capital from 2014 to 2018.
- **Priya Iyer** — CFO. Joined Acme in 2021. Formerly Treasurer at
  Salesforce.
- **Hiroshi Tanaka** — CTO. Joined Acme in 2017. Formerly principal
  engineer at AWS.
- **Linda Okafor** — Chief Revenue Officer. Joined Acme in 2020.
  Formerly VP Sales at Snowflake.
- **Marcus Webb** — General Counsel. Joined Acme in 2018.
